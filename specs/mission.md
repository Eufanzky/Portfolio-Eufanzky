# Mission

## Purpose

This portfolio is where the user shows their work as a software developer. Its main job is to help the user get hired: a recruiter or hiring manager should quickly see what the user has built, what they know, and how to contact them.

## Audience

**Main audience: recruiters and hiring managers.** They often look on a phone, have little time, and decide quickly. Every section should help them answer three questions:

1. What has the user built recently? (Projects)
2. What can the user do, and where have they done it? (Experience and Tech)
3. How do I contact the user? (Contact)

## Principles

- **Current over complete.** The projects shown must reflect the user's most recent and strongest work. Outdated or weak projects are removed or moved lower, not kept for volume.
- **Fast on every device.** The site must load and scroll smoothly on a mid-range phone. When visual effects conflict with speed on mobile, speed wins.
- **Proof, not claims.** Each project links to a live demo and/or its source code, and states its tech stack and the user's role in it.
- **Honest, professional descriptions.** Each project description matches the real work done: what was actually built, the user's actual role, and real results. Nothing is exaggerated (no inflated scope, users or impact). The wording is still clear, concise and professional.
- **Easy to contact.** The contact form always works, and contact options are easy to find from any section.

## Success criteria

- Projects section lists the user's current projects, each with a description, tech tags, image, and working links.
- Lighthouse mobile performance score of 90 or higher on the production site.
- No broken links, and the contact form sends email in production.
